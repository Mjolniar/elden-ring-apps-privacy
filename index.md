# Privacy Policy

**Apps:** Elden Ring Index and Build Planner; Elden Ring Randomizer Index and Build Planner

**Last updated:** 2026-10-30 (wording only; practices unchanged since 2026-05-10)

*Written with AI.*

## Summary

No personal data collected. The apps run on your device. Nothing is sent to any server.

## Stored on your device

| Data | Location | Purpose |
|------|----------|---------|
| Starred items | localStorage | Keep stars between sessions |
| Acquired items | localStorage | Keep collected marks |
| Spoiler mode settings | localStorage | Keep Settings tab choices |
| Custom builds | localStorage | Keep your build checklists |
| Starred build presets | localStorage | Keep starred presets |
| Last spoiler log *(Randomizer edition only)* | File next to the executable | Restore the session on next launch |

Not transmitted, synced or backed up anywhere.

## Not done

- No network requests
- No server or backend
- No analytics or usage data
- No cookies
- No tracking identifiers
- No reading, changing or interacting with Elden Ring files or the running game
- No background services or auto-updaters

## Links

Item names link to [eldenring.wiki.fextralife.com](https://eldenring.wiki.fextralife.com). They open in your browser
only when clicked. That site's privacy policy applies there.

## Spoiler logs *(Randomizer edition only)*

You load a `.txt` spoiler log from your computer. It is read locally and never sent. A copy is cached in
`cached-spoiler-logs\` next to the executable to restore the session. Clear it with **Load new log**.

## Children

No data is collected from anyone.

Updates are posted at this URL with a new date.
